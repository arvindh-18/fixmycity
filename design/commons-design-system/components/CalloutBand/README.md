# CalloutBand

The one tinted moment on a page: a full-width `sage-100` band, square-cornered, with a serif heading on the left and an illustration that breaks out above and below the band on the right.

- Consumer provides: `title` (italicise one phrase with `<em>`), `children` (one short paragraph), optional `action` (usually a primary Button) and optional `art` (an illustration or framed icon).
- One band per page. Padding is `space-12`; the art overhangs by `space-16`, so leave `space-16` of air above and below the band.
