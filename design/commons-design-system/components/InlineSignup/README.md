# InlineSignup

A centred email field and primary button on one line, with a consent checkbox under it — the brand's way to ask for updates.

- Consumer provides: `cta` (default “Subscribe”), `placeholder`, `consent` (a node, usually with a Privacy Policy link) and `onSubmit(email)`.
- Use at the top and bottom of landing pages and under a report (“Get updates on this report”). Don't ask for anything but an email here.
