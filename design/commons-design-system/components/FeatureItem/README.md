# FeatureItem

An icon, a bold serif title and two lines of muted copy; set in a row of three or four to explain how the service works.

- Consumer provides: `icon` (an `<img>` from the Icons group at 64px), `title`, and `children` (the description, one or two sentences in `body-sm`).
- Titles are two or three words, sentence case: “Snap and send”.
- Columns separate with `space-8`; no borders, no cards, no background.
